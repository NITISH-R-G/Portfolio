Why we moved off Python

By Ines Baptista · 11 February 2026

For six years our data pipeline was Python end to end. We used Pandas for transformation, Airflow for scheduling, and PyTorch for the ranking model. Kubernetes held it together.

What changed

Rust and Go both came up. The team had experience with neither. Amazon's SDK made the decision easier than expected, and Snowflake absorbed most of the transformation work that Pandas had been doing.

What I would do differently

Less TypeScript in the tooling layer. More SQL. The Kafka migration could have waited a year.
